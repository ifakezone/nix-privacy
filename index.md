# Nix Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Nix checks webpages you approve for keywords and phrases you choose. It can display desktop notifications and optionally refresh approved pages at your selected interval.

## Data accessed and stored

Nix reads webpage text locally to detect keyword matches. It stores your keywords, approved websites, refresh settings, and up to 30 recent detections in your browser. Each detection includes the matching keyword, page title, page URL, and detection time.

Temporary session data prevents duplicate alerts and connects notifications to their source tabs.

## Data sharing

Nix does not transmit webpage content, settings, or detection history to its developer or third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, or remote executable code.

When auto-refresh is enabled, your browser makes normal requests to the website being refreshed.

## Your controls

You can edit keywords, remove approved websites, disable auto-refresh, or pause monitoring. Uninstalling Nix removes its local extension data.
